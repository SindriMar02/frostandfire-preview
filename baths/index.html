Baths

Warm waterfrom our own borehole

Hot tubs on the riverside, a warm lap pool and a black stone sauna. Included with every room.

Hot tubs

On the river,under the sky

There is nothing like the outdoor hot tubs after a day of hiking. They sit right on the riverside, warmed by geothermal water from the hotel's own borehole. Under the midnight sun or with snow on the ground and the northern lights overhead.

  • IncludedWith every room, during opening hours
  • WaterGeothermal, from the hotel's own borehole
  • Pool12 metres, may close in bad weather
  • DoorsEvery room has its own entrance to the baths

Swimming pool

Twelve metres,warm all year

The warmed lap pool is 12 metres long, with the mountains all around. Like the tubs it is fed from the hotel's own borehole. It can close in bad weather.

Sauna

Black stoneand wooden benches

The quintessential Nordic way to recharge for the next outing.

The warm river

Varmá meanswarm river

Hot springs empty into the riverbed, and in summer the water can reach around 18 °C. Wading out into the Varmá on a dark night is an experience of its own. For the warmest water, walk up to Reykjadalur: the trail starts about 2 km from the hotel.
