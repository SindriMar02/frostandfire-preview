Restaurant Varmá

Dinner overthe warm river

Breakfast every morning, dinner in the glass room in the evening.

The name

Named afterthe river

The restaurant takes its name from the Varmá, the warm river below its windows. Views of the river and its gentle current are one of the highlights of the rooms and the restaurant.

Breakfast

Bread fromthe hot spring

Hverabrauð, a rich, moist rye bread, is baked overnight in the hotel's own ancient hot spring. With smoked trout from North Iceland, organic produce from the geothermal greenhouses and eggs from free range Icelandic hens, which guests may boil in the hot spring themselves.
